Bandit

Hold up 5 people

Fairly simple, just aim your gun at a random civilian and rob them (repeat 5 times) Rob 2 Coaches You’ll see coaches out in the open world fairly frequently, and you may even get a campquest to go rob one with a friend. Either kill or hold up the drivers, and take whatever loot is in the back. Another method in you don't want to go on the prowl and you happen to have the lockbreaker, is to silently rob the fast travel coach (yes you can do that), fast travel with the fast travel coach, and then rob it again. Rob the cash register in 4 shops in 1 day Sleep at the strawberry inn, and start by robbing the general store first thing in the morning. From there, ride your way down to Wallace Station, and do the same at the general store there. Now ride all the way down to Valentine, hold up the general store there, and then make your way down to rhoades, and at this point you could assume what to do there. Rob three coaches in 1 day I’ve already explained how to rob coaches, and there isn’t really an optimal spawn point for them, so I recommend prowling the heartland trails and robbing every coach you see. Get a 250$ bounty This is a fun one as there's a way to do this without losing any honor (if you even want the high honor ending). Go to Saint Denis and find a nice hiding spot (can be an alley or rooftop; preferably a rooftop). Now, shoot at someone (not purposefully hitting them), if no one is around, shooting at the street also works. If all goes well, you’ll be reported as disturbing the peace, and an officer will come along and attempt to deal with you. Shoot them in the face and prepare to go guns blazing on any cop that dares face you down. Since cops are registered enemies, you won't be losing any honor by mercilessly killing them. Amass a fat 250$ bounty, and get outta there (paying it off is optional but I recommend it if you wanna be in that area in peace again) Steal and sell horses (chapter 3 and beyond) This part can only be done post chapter 3, meaning you cannot finish the bandit challenges in chapter 2. Once you unlock the horse fence however, this becomes very simple as you can just steal people's horses on the road nearby and bring them to the fence. Rob 50$ off of townsfolk and travelers Remember robbing people? Do that a lot. Steal and sell 5 wagons In chapter 2, you should've unlocked the wagon fence from a main mission early on. This one is also fairly simple, find wagons out on the road or stationary in a town, and yoink it. Repeat 5 times. Hogtie someone and leave them on train tracks a total of 3 times Now this is the stereotypical “Wild West” type scenario, and the good news is you don’t have to wait for the train. Simply tie whoever you want up, toss them on the tracks, and walk away. Rob five trains without dying or being arrested This one isn't really hard, just time consuming. Go to a train station, and buy a ticket. When on the train, go to the car at the very rear and take something. Jump off. Do this 4 more times without dying or being arrested and you’ve officially become a master bandit.

Gambler

Win five hands of Poker I honestly don't know how to play poker, I just know face cards are good and low cards are bad. But I did manage to wing it through all of the poker sections of the game fairly easily In blackjack, double down and Win the hand five times This challenge is decently luck based, so you mainly have to pray that you get the right cards to double down and win (also don’t put too much money down, you may be at this for a while) Win three games of Five Finger Filet This one is actually pretty fun, as it’s significantly more skill based than the other ones, go to any FFF game, and let those quick time events rip. If you’re even a little bit fast, odds are you’ll win. Bust somebody out at each poker location (Valentine, Saint Denis, Blackwater, and Tumbleweed, plus at Flatneck Station) Basically, play well enough to get someone out of the game at each location. Fairly simple. Win three round of dominoes without drawing any tiles against 1-2 opponents This one is a little tricky as drawing tiles is common practice in dominoes, so you kinda just have to pray the odds are in your favor Beat the blackjack dealer in 2 locations (Rhodes and Van Horn) This ones pretty simple. Win. Twice. Beat the Five Finger Filet at least once at every Location (Strawberry, Valentine, and Van Horn) At every location there will be someone sitting at the table waiting to play. Beat each one once. Win 3 games of blackjack with three hits or more More praying! Yay! Listen to music and spam hits and buy in. Don't buy in for too much, you’ll be at this for a while Win 3 games of dominoes in a row Also self explanatory, you got this champ. Win 3 hands of poker in a row Mane fuck
